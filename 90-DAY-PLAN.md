# 90-Day Plan — Agency + Dr Evide

**Owner:** Shakir · **Start:** Aug 11, 2026 · **End:** Nov 8, 2026
**Split:** 3 days/week agency · 2 days/week Dr Evide · review the split monthly.

---

## Part 1 — The Agency (income engine)

**Positioning:** Premium animated websites + AI assistants for clinics, colleges, and Gulf-market Malayali businesses. Not "web developer" — *"I build sites that look world-class and answer your customers' questions automatically."*

**Pricing (hold the line):**

| Offer | Price |
|---|---|
| Landing/brand site (animated, mobile-first) | ₹40k–₹70k |
| Full site + booking/forms | ₹80k–₹1.5L |
| AI assistant add-on (RAG chatbot on their content) | +₹25k setup |
| Retainer (hosting, updates, chatbot maintenance) | ₹5k–₹15k/month |

**Income target:** ₹50k/month average by Day 90 = ~2 projects + 3–4 retainers.

### Days 1–30 — Build the storefront
- [ ] Register the business (proprietorship or LLP later; get a current account + UPI)
- [ ] Build your own agency site — this IS the portfolio piece. Awwwards-style, scroll-driven, with a live AI assistant on it. One week, timeboxed.
- [ ] Create 2 spec demos: a fictional clinic site + a fictional restaurant/store site, each with a working chatbot. These are your sales weapons.
- [ ] Write a one-page offer PDF in Malayalam + English (what you build, prices, 3 screenshots, WhatsApp number)
- [ ] List 50 prospects: clinics/labs in Edappal–Tirur belt, MES/college networks, Gulf-Malayali businesses (Instagram + Kudumbashree/business WhatsApp groups)
- **Milestone Day 30:** agency site live, 2 demos live, 50-prospect list done.

### Days 31–60 — Sell
- [ ] Outreach rhythm: 10 contacts/week — 5 WhatsApp/DM, 3 in-person clinic visits, 2 referral asks. In-person converts best in Kerala; lead with the demo on your phone.
- [ ] Offer the first 2 clients a "founding client" price (~30% off) in exchange for a testimonial + case study
- [ ] Deliver fast: 2-week max per project. Use your fx-scope/fx-plan/fx-ship workflow to keep scope tight.
- [ ] Every delivery ends with the retainer pitch: "₹7,500/month and I keep it fast, updated, and the chatbot current."
- **Milestone Day 60:** 2 paid projects delivered or in progress, 1 retainer signed.

### Days 61–90 — Systemize
- [ ] Publish both case studies (before/after, load speed, enquiries generated)
- [ ] Raise prices back to full rate for client #3 onward
- [ ] Template your build: reusable scaffold + chatbot pipeline so a site takes 1 week, not 2
- [ ] Ask every client for 2 referrals — referrals should be ≥50% of pipeline by Day 90
- **Milestone Day 90:** ₹40–60k earned cumulatively, 3+ retainers, repeatable delivery process.

## Part 2 — Dr Evide (product bet)

**The real work is supply, not code.** The app exists; the moat is verified doctors that no one else has listed honestly.

**North-star metric:** weekly real searches that end in a doctor contact/visit intent.

**Kill/continue gate (Day 180, set now):** 20+ verified doctors AND 500+ real searches/month AND at least anecdotal evidence patients found a doctor through it. Miss all three → fold the tech into the agency as a "find-a-doctor widget" for hospital clients.

### Days 1–30 — Supply groundwork
- [ ] Freeze features. Only bug fixes + whatever doctor onboarding requires. No new code otherwise.
- [ ] Build the doctor onboarding flow: a simple form + NMC registration number verification checklist (manual is fine at this scale)
- [ ] List every clinic/hospital in Edappal, Ponnani, Kuttippuram, Valanchery, Tirur — target 60–80 doctors identified
- [ ] Visit 10 clinics in person. Pitch: *"Free listing, ranked only by credentials and real reviews — we never sell ranking. Verify your NMC number and you're live."* The no-paid-ranking CI check is your credibility line — show it.
- [ ] Recruit 2–3 "anchor" respected doctors first; their names pull others in
- **Milestone Day 30:** 8–10 verified doctors live.

### Days 31–60 — Demand test
- [ ] Soft launch to real users: college network, family WhatsApp groups, local Facebook groups — in Malayalam
- [ ] One distribution experiment/week: Malayalam reel ("എവിടെ നല്ല skin doctor?" → app demo), pharmacy poster with QR, auto-stand QR, local WhatsApp status pushes
- [ ] Instrument everything: searches, specialty routed, results clicked, dead-ends (specialty with no doctors = next recruiting target)
- [ ] Talk to 15 real users. One question matters: *did you actually go to the doctor you found?*
- **Milestone Day 60:** 15+ verified doctors, 200+ real searches, 3+ confirmed "found my doctor here" stories.

### Days 61–90 — Double down or adjust
- [ ] Fill the gaps: recruit doctors in the top 3 searched-but-empty specialties
- [ ] Ship the ONE feature users actually asked for (likely: phone-call button, timings, or Malayalam UI polish) — nothing else
- [ ] Test the future business model quietly: would clinics pay ₹2–5k/month for a verified profile page + appointment requests? (Payment must never touch ranking — that's the brand.) Ask 5 clinics, don't build anything yet.
- [ ] Write a 1-page Day-90 review: metrics vs. the Day-180 gate, on track or not
- **Milestone Day 90:** 20 verified doctors, 400+ searches/month, clear read on willingness-to-pay.
